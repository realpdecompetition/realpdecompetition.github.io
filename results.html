Method

How the scores were obtained

Teams
Among the teams that submitted a valid Decision Phase training package (training code and Docker image) and remained eligible under the competition rules, each track ranks the 10 placed highest on that track's Development Phase leaderboard. Teams are listed by the team name they gave with their training package, followed by their Codabench account in parentheses where the two differ.
Training
Each team submitted a separate training package for each track it competes in. The organizers trained each package from scratch three times on the organizers' NVIDIA A800 GPUs, which gives three trained models per team and track.
Evaluation
Each trained model was evaluated five times on the organizers' NVIDIA A800 GPUs, the same GPU type as for the Development Phase leaderboard. The evaluation followed the Development Phase protocol, run on the private test set instead of the Development Phase evaluation data. The five subscores were combined into a total score in the same way as for the leaderboard's final score.
Total score
The table shows the mean total score over the team's 15 evaluations in that track (3 trained models × 5 evaluations each) and the standard deviation over the same 15 evaluations. Higher is better.
Shared places
When two adjacent teams' mean scores differ by less than both teams' standard deviations, the evaluation cannot separate them, and they share the place, marked with =. Three places are shared:
  • Track 1, 6th place: skabob and AxisTilted2 (spsanps).
  • Track 2, 3rd place: AxisTilted2 (spsanps) and np-user.
  • Track 2, 9th place: AI4Silence (gaswang) and Cupid (andychang).

What each place receives is listed under Prizes. Questions about the results: realpde-competition@googlegroups.com.
